# The cast pool

Twelve present actors — eleven people and the dog. The stranger enters on
`someone.is-asking`. Casting needs nine to twelve or `prefer` is round-robin.

## Mortality

The supply (`HORRORS.md` §5) makes the cast mortal on every run — slowly while
it is never named, fast once it is.

- The coverage table is a starting position, not a property of the pool.
- `is-gone` ≠ `has-stopped-coming`. One avoids a place; the other is a hole in
  every future deal, including mid-thread and including sole candidates.
- Twelve becomes the right number rather than a comfortable one.

## What the pool does not decide

Three readings survive any run, and no situation may assert one:

1. The ring was Anna's and she gave it back.
2. Anselm has been protecting his nephew since.
3. Nothing here is local — the coat, the whistle carried a long way, and the
   knife the only thing anyone recognises.

> **Facts record what was said and done. No fact records what is true about the
> death.**
