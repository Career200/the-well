# The curve

How escalation is written. `HORRORS.md` says what the escalation is *about*.

## Two laws

> **Closure opens.** Every fact that removes content names what it makes
> possible; every band ceiling has some situation's floor at or above it. Both
> are static checks: a fact appearing only in `none:` clauses is a pure
> subtraction, and a ceiling that opens onto nothing is a hole in the run.

> **The worst thing happens before the cut.** Dread truncates after beat 2.
> Content saving its worst for beat 4 disappears exactly when the run is most
> frightening. The escalated material goes in the opening — sound before sight,
> and the sound is the bad part. Later beats carry consequence, not climax.

The second inverts ordinary scene construction. A scene built to a beat-4 climax
plays best in the calmest run.

## Channel 1 — the same press, worse

`Affordance.when` carries a Gate, so an affordance is written three times against
dread bands at prose cost only. No new input; what changes is what the lever
means.

```
explained away   they have a reason for it, and they use it
not explained    they have stopped having a reason and have not said so
not yours        it happens without you
```

Water at the rim:
```
explained away   The water moves. She looks at it and decides it was the bucket.
not explained    The water moves. She does not look at it. She stopped looking at
                 it at some point without telling herself she had.
not yours        You are still. The water is not.
```

Cold, on a hold:
```
explained away   It goes cold at the rim and she puts her hands under her arms.
not explained    It goes cold and she does not react, because reacting is how a
                 thing gets talked about.
not yours        It is cold before you take anything up. You have not decided
                 which one yet.
```

The dog:
```
explained away   The dog's head comes up.
not explained    The dog is already looking at the place you are about to be.
```

`not yours` is the point: the haunting outgrows the haunter. No capability is
gained — a new power is a new verb in disguise. Ownership is lost.

## Channel 2 — the cut is an event

Already budgeted, one line per situation. Respecified:

> The cut-short line names what did the cutting, and it is never the person's
> good manners.

```
wrong   She does not finish. Nobody finishes anything here now.
right   She gets two words out. The stone takes the rest of it the way the water
        takes a dropped thing.
```

It may be the place, the cold, the light, the rope. It may not be the well doing
something the player did not pay for: the dread they stand in is theirs,
delayed.

## Channel 3 — one more Below block

Not the dread-as-prose-axis that `DIALS.md` §7 rejected. Six subjects, one extra
paragraph each, printed above the dread band on top of the current lucidity tier
— additive, so the readable rule survives: *more text means worse.*

```ts
dread?: string   // six subjects, not eighteen
```

**The water, the cold, the walls, the body, the rope, the sky.** The belongings
get none — they run on lucidity and stay the one channel about recognition
rather than fear.

```
the water   It has not been still since the second time. You have stopped
            waiting for it to be.
the walls   You know every one of these stones now. That took less time than you
            would have thought.
the body    You have started to check on it. You do not know what you are
            checking for.
the sky     The coin of sky is the same size. You have checked.
```

~300 words. It is the only escalation the player feels inside the run, downward,
where they are always looking.

## Channel 4 — a thread that worsens

One thread, `onMiss: silent` throughout, advancing on its own clock: the water
rising, the searching, the thing that comes up. Three of the twenty slots, not
an addition to them.

## The narrator loses its irony

The tiers carry a second progression, written into their definitions rather than
left to instinct:

```
veiled   wry, and does not know why. Distance as reflex
plain    the jokes stop landing for the narrator first. Observations without the
         turn at the end of them
named    no distance at all. Short sentences. Nothing is being handled
```

Costs nothing — a constraint on the existing ~2,500 words of `Below`, and the
largest tonal lever in the project. A presence still making neat observations in
the last minute undercuts everything the run built.

## Guardrails

- **Escalation is never volume.** Louder is not worse; more specific is worse.
- **No capability is ever gained.** The input surface is closed forever.
- **Nothing is confirmed on the way up.** The pressure to explain grows as the
  run worsens and must be refused every time.
- **The village never becomes stupid.** Closing up reads as competent
  self-protection.
- **Nobody becomes a monster.** The throwing is men doing a job they talked
  themselves into. Tomas at his worst is a man.
- **Warmth is spent, not deleted.** `unguarded` must still work at dread 0.9.
- **Luck is not the absence of cost; it is getting to choose whose.**
- A dog is one warm adjective from wrecking the register, and that generalises
  to Sev, the Ferrin house, and every scene with a child in it.
