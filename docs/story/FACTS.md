# The fact dictionary

Fifty facts. The brief asked 25–40; §9 is the overage, and it exists
because the horrors became three roads and a supply. Every track is live every
run, but only the one that ramps sets most of its facts, so per-run load is
roughly unchanged.

**Conventions.** A bare dotted string is a world fact (`well.boarded`). An
unqualified string is a person fact, set through a role and queried unqualified
during casting (`unguarded`). `≈` = read as weather by unrelated situations.
`→` = what a closure opens.

**Laws.** Every fact has two or more origins. Closure opens — a fact appearing
only in `none:` clauses is a pure subtraction and the lint says so. Facts record
what was said and done, never what is true about the death.

≈                 from  two at the rim agree it without deciding it

## Appendix — audit

Two origins only (the floor): `well.sounded`, `village.gathered`,
`someone.went-in`, `knows-the-body`, `water.high`, `water.low`,
`coat.out-of-reach`. All plot-adjacent, where thin is acceptable.

Wants a third most: `village.gathered` (leans on a high gate a quiet run may
never reach) · `knows-the-body` (all three routes run through the same lamp).

**The deck these origins imply:**

```
someone comes for water *      the dog at the rim †      the sounding
two at the rim *               asking after the dog †    the hearing
children at the rim *          the owner calls †         the blessing
the carter passes *            the meeting place ×3      the ruling
a child sent alone             Tomas alone, after dark   the throwing
a mother comes for her child   the outsider asks         after the boards
Bern works at the rim          the search party ‡
```

`†` the tutorial thread. `‡` one of three slots reserved for the worsening
thread. `*` barometer candidates — each an origin for five or more facts, each
plausible from an empty world to a closed-up one, each survives being cut to one
beat.

Twenty-plus against a ceiling of twenty, before the eight road-lens situations.
Deliverable 3 is pruning, and severe. When a new situation suggests itself, ask
which existing one it is a casting of.
