### beats

**1 — what is heard**

`wary` — > Feet on the track, and then not on the track. Somebody has come off
> it into the grass, on the side where the houses cannot see this wall. Then
> nothing, for a long time. Whoever it is, they are not doing anything.

`plain` — > Feet, straight up, at the pace a person walks to a thing they have
> to do before dark. They stop at the wall the way you stop at a gate.

`tender` — > Feet, slowly, and a voice before the feet get here — not saying
> anything to anybody, just the sound a person makes coming up a track on their
> own, on and off, the way you do.

**2 — what is seen through the coin of sky**

> The light breaks and does not come back. They are over the wall and they stay
> over it. They are not looking at the wall, where the thing is. They are
> looking down, and they go on looking down, and the light stays broken for as
> long as that takes.

`tender` — > The light breaks and does not come back. They have got both
> forearms flat on the stone and their head right over, the way you lean on a
> gate to talk to somebody in a field. They are looking down. They stay there.

**3 — the turn**

`wary` — > They come back up and look at the track, both ways, and then they
> put a hand on the thing on the wall and move it about a finger's width, and
> then look at the track again.

`plain` — > They pick it up, turn it over, and put it back down on the flat of
> the stone with the good side up. They brush the top of the wall off with the
> side of their hand, all the way along, the way you would a shelf.

`tender` — > They say something. It is not long and it is not loud and the
> shaft takes all of it. It is the sort of thing that gets said to somebody who
> is ill, in the voice you use when the answer is going to be a while coming.

**4 — the moment it could still go either way**

> They have not gone. There is nothing else out here to be doing and they are
> still here, and the light in the coin of sky has moved off the wall and onto
> the water.

with `the-other` — > There is somebody else on the track now, and they have
> stopped, and they are far enough off that neither of them has to be the one
> who speaks first.

### affords

**press → the water answers**   beats 2–4 · cost high

> below — You move. It is the smallest thing you have done all day. The shaft
> takes it and makes it into a sound with a shape on it and hands it up through
> the hole to the person leaning in.

> above, `wary` — They are up and three steps back before the sound has
> finished. Then they stop. Then they come back.

> above, `plain` — They stop straightening it.

> above, `tender` — They do not move. After a while they say thank you.

> late, `not yours` — The water goes before you do. They are already saying it.

**hold ⟨belonging⟩ → it is cold where they are kneeling**   beats 1–4 · cost
that belonging's charge, per beat

> below — You hold on to the thing and the cold comes off it the way it always
> does, and this time it goes up, past the wet line on the stones, and stops
> where the person is, and stays around them like something standing close.

> above, `wary` — They straighten up and put their hands under their arms and
> look at the sky to see whether the weather has turned. It has not.

> above, `plain` — They go on with what they were doing and do not react,
> because reacting is how a thing gets talked about.

> above, `tender` — They put their coat over the wall first, and then their
> arms on the coat, and settle down onto it.

**still**

> Nothing happens. They wait, and nothing happens, and then they move it to
> where the stone is flat. They turn it so that the good side is up. They take
> a second thing out of a pocket and set it down beside the first, and it is
> not much, and they look at the two of them for a moment before they go.

what it is for     it makes the well answerable — `well.answered` is what every
                   later step on this road gates on, and this is the scene it
                   comes out of. It is also where somebody starts being out
                   here at night, which changes the hours anything else can
                   happen at.

what it costs      it closes its own gate. `someone.keeps-watch` puts a person
                   in the coin of sky at hours it has never had one, and coming
                   back on your own to look stops being possible, because there
                   is now always somebody to see you do it. What that opens is
                   the second person, and after that nobody is ever first.
